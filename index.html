Who I am

Hi, my name is Dima. I am a guy who started learning programming at the age of 14. My journey started with a Udemy course about python programming and ever since I began making my own projects during free time. I have mostly learnt on my own, building websites, arduinos, simple AIs, automation software, back-ends and more. Over the years I have learnt quite a lot and despite great risks of the AI era, I chose to keep building and learning unable to wait to see where my skills will take me.

My principles

Ever since I started, I have been very passionate about designing and building software. This made me approach every project with three main principles:

  • Understanding before abstracting
  • Follow good practice
  • Never stop learning

Those principles have been relevant before AI and will most likely stay forever. Following them made me develop quality applications and achieve continuous growth in the production of software and my personal life.

How I approach Projects and Problems

I frequently dream big about some of my projects before putting them into existence. And for big tasks and problems it becomes important to break them down into smaller pieces and solve them one after another. If I face a new big project, I will take my time finding the right ground system. Whether I choose a framework, a library or build it myself isn't crucial. What matters is to pick a system that works best in the specific situation with all the applying factors. From there it's pretty simple, build the tool, feature by feature, commit by commit while networking with your teammates.
